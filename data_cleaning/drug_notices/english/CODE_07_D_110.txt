## Pharmacodynamics

Amorolfine is an antifungal agent with fungistatic and fungicidal activity in vitro. 1

## Mechanism Of Action

Amorolfine mediates its antifungal effects by interfering with ergosterol biosynthesis in the fungal cell membrane by inhibiting delta(14)-sterol reductase and cholestenol delta-isomerase. 4 It alters the membrane sterol content, thereby changing the membrane permeability and disrupting metabolic processes. 2 , 3 Target Actions Organism A Delta(14)-sterol reductase ERG24 inhibitor Baker's yeast A C-8 sterol isomerase ERG2 inhibitor Baker's yeast U Sodium channel protein type 7 subunit alpha blocker Humans
