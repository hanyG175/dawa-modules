## Description

An antifungal antibiotic. Griseofulvin may be given by mouth in the treatment of tinea infections.

## Pharmacodynamics

Griseofulvin is a mycotoxic metabolic product of Penicillium spp. It was the first available oral agent for the treatment of dermatophytoses and has now been used

## Mechanism Of Action

Tubulin beta chain (Trichophyton rubrum) Inhibitor

## Absorption

Poorly absorbed from GI ranging from 25 to 70% of an oral dose. Absorption is significantly enhanced by administration with or after a fatty meal.

## Metabolism

Primarily hepatic with major metabolites being 6-methyl-griseofulvin and its glucuronide conjugate. Hover over products below to view reaction partners Griseofulvin 6-methyl-griseofulvin

## Half Life

9-21 hours

## Toxicity

Side effects are minor: headaches, gastrointestinal reactions and cutaneous eruptions.
