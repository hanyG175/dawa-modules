## Description

Niflumic acid is an analgesic and anti-inflammatory agent used in the treatment of rheumatoid arthritis.

## Pharmacodynamics

Niflumic acid, a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory fenamate, is a Ca 2+ -activated Cl - channel blocker.

## Mechanism Of Action

Phospholipase A2 Inhibitor Prostaglandin G/H synthase 2 Inhibitor

## Absorption

Well absorbed following oral administration.

## Metabolism

Hepatic.

## Half Life

2.5 hours

## Toxicity

Oral, mouse: LD 50 = 350 mg/kg; Oral, rat: LD 50 = 250 mg/kg
