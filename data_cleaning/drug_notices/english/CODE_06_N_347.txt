## Indications

Riociguat is indicated for the treatment of adults with persistent/recurrent chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension (CTEPH), (WHO Group 4) after surgical treatment, or inoperable CTEPH, to improve exercise capacity and WHO functional class.

## Mechanism Of Action

Guanylate cyclase soluble subunit alpha-2 Agonist Stimulator

## Absorption

The pharmacokinetics of riociguant are dose proportional from 0.5 mg to 2.5 mg. The absolute bioavailability is approximately 94%. After oral administration, peak plasma concentrations were achieved within 1.5 hours. Food does not affect the bioavailability of riociguat.

## Volume Of Distribution

Volume of distribution at steady state = 30 L

## Metabolism

The active metabolite (M1) of riociguat is 1/3 to 1/10 as potent as riociguat.

## Route Of Elimination

Riociguat is eliminated in the urine (40%) and feces (53%), largely as metabolites.

## Half Life

About 12 hours in patients and 7 hours in healthy subjects.

## Toxicity

EMBRYO-FETAL TOXICITY
Do not administer Riociguat to a pregnant female because it may cause fetal harm.
Females of reproductive potential: Exclude pregnancy before the start of treatment, monthly during treatment, and 1 month after stopping treatment. Prevent pregnancy during treatment and for one month after stopping treatment by using acceptable methods of contraception.
For all female patients, Riociguat is available only through a restricted program called the Adempas Risk Evaluation and Mitigation Strategy.
